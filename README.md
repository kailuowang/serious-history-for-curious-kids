# Serious History for Curious Kids — *The Mechanics of Civilization*

A 42-chapter (~500-page) history of human civilization written for an analytically sharp 9-year-old reader: how societies build rules and politics, how economies and money work, and how monumental engineering solves stubborn physical problems—explained in concrete everyday language with zero condescension and zero unexplained jargon.

## Core Principles

1. **Adult Mechanisms, Plain Words:** Teach real structural mechanics (*surplus, politics, tax, ledger, contract, credit, load-bearing physics*) through concrete physical puzzles before naming them.
2. **Strict Source Grounding:** Every number, date, ration, dimension, and historical debate traces directly to verified scholarly notes in `sources/` and `research/`.
3. **Unfolding-Mystery Flow:** Every chapter and section moves through a tight causal chain (*Physical Problem → Clues & Constraints → Mechanism & Payoff → Next Problem*), checked by an independent Flow Reviewer.
4. **Visual Craftsmanship:** Every chapter pairs prose with 4–6 custom, visually verified vector plates (`diagrams/`) and curated open-licensed museum & real-world photographs (`photos/`).
